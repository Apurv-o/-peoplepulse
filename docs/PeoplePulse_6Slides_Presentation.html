Methodology & Design Philosophy

Our Approach: From Passive Measurement to Closed-Loop Action

Pillar 1: Data & Privacy

60s Pulses & Privacy Invariants

Replaces painful annual surveys with 60-second calibrated check-ins. Enforces mathematical anonymity at the database kernel:

• is_anonymous = true ➔ user_id IS NULL
• Single-use UUID token consumed atomically
• n ≥ 3 aggregation barrier enforced via RPC

Zero tracking of IP addresses, browser agents, or machine fingerprints.

PulseAgent attempts to broadcast an urgent alert to the Support manager via an external team webhook (Slack/Teams). The endpoint times out:

HTTP/1.1 503 Service Unavailable (Timeout > 3000ms)

What Fragile Bots Do: Crash unhandled, drop the notification, and leave management completely blind.

AUTONOMOUS RECOVERY

Self-Healing Failure Adaptation

PulseAgent intercepts the failure within its execution boundary and adapts its strategy autonomously:

1. Intercept: Catches HTTP 503 cleanly without crashing.
2. Adapt: Emits Glowing Orange Adaptation Card in timeline.
3. Reroute: Dispatches to Supabase internal emergency queue.
